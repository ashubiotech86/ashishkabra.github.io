About Me

Research Scientist specializing in structural biology, biomolecular NMR spectroscopy, biophysics, and structure-guided drug discovery. Focus areas include the characterization of protein dynamic networks, allosteric pathways, dynamic target interactions, and macromolecular condensates to guide small-molecule inhibitor design in target-driven cancer therapeutics.

Core Competencies

Biomolecular NMR Spectroscopy NMR Relaxation Analysis Structural Biology Cancer Biology Structure-Guided Drug Screening Protein Allostery & Dynamics 3D Scientific Visualization & Modeling

Education & Academic Credentials

  • Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) in Structural Biology — CSIR-Central Drug Research Institute (CSIR-CDRI), Lucknow, India
  • Master of Science (MSc) in Marine Biotechnology — Goa University, Goa, India
  • ACS Peer Reviewer Certificate — Recognized peer reviewer for American Chemical Society journals

Research Focus & Projects

  • Allosteric Dynamics & Signal Transduction: Investigation of fast and slow conformational dynamics in transcription factors and protein domains using NMR relaxation experiments to decipher functional dynamic networks.
  • Structure-Guided Drug Discovery: Combining structural, biophysical, and computational methodologies to identify and optimize therapeutic inhibitors against dynamic drug targets.
  • Biomolecular Condensates & 3D Modeling: Structural analysis and high-fidelity 3D rendering of macromolecular condensates and protein systems.
