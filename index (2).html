About me

Research-driven structural biology with an emphasis on mechanism and molecular recognition.

My research focuses on understanding the molecular mechanisms that control transcription factors and their interactions with proteins, DNA, RNA, and small molecules. I use complementary biophysical and structural approaches to connect molecular interactions with biological function.

My work spans cancer-relevant targets and therapeutic discovery, with particular emphasis on protein dynamics, ligand binding, structure–activity relationships, and mechanistic characterization.
